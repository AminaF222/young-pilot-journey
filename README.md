Young Pilot Journey

Young Pilot Journey is an educational aviation app designed to help young people explore aviation and learn about the journey toward becoming a pilot.

The app provides aspiring pilots with helpful information, educational resources, and interactive activities to encourage their interest in aviation.

What the App Offers

Aviation Education: Learn about aviation and the steps involved in pursuing a pilot license.
Your First Flight: An interactive aviation game where users explore flight-related decisions and earn points and badges.
Pilot Alphabet: Learn aviation communication terminology.
Flight Opportunities: Discover organizations and programs that offer young people opportunities to experience aviation.
STEM Resources: Explore educational opportunities that connect science, technology, engineering, mathematics, and aviation.
Weather Information: Access weather information as part of learning about aviation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make aviation education more accessible to young people, encourage exploration of STEM careers, and help aspiring pilots take their first steps toward the sky.

Young Pilot Journey was created to inspire the next generation of aviators.
